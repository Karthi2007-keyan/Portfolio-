Who I Am

About Me

I learn by building. Not by watching tutorials or reading theory alone — by actually writing code, breaking things, and figuring out how to fix them.

I'm a Diploma student at Sri Ramakrishna Polytechnic College with a growing focus on Artificial Intelligence and full-stack development. Over the past couple of years, I've built ten practical software projects across web development, AI/NLP, computer vision, and system design.

From an AI-powered resume ranking system that uses NLP concepts to match candidates to job descriptions, to an AI Music Mentor that analyzes pitch and rhythm, to a real-time webcam-based virtual try-on e-commerce platform — each project taught me something the classroom couldn't.

I've also competed at technical events and won three 2nd-prize awards, completed an internship on AI-Powered Integrated System Design, and received an Academic Excellence Award for consistent academic performance.
